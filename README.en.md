# CVIII: Content and Visualization Index with Intelligence Involved 
![CVIII-*1](https://img.shields.io/badge/CVIII-*1-D9A295)

> [!NOTE]  
> The current proposal is a draft amendment based on IIIA, provided for reference only.  
> The original author hasn't officially adopted this proposal yet and hasn't responded either. If the original author responds, this proposal will update as appropriate. 

[IIIA](https://github.com/ErSanSan233/IIIA) (<span lang="fr">**I**ndice d'**i**mplication de l'**i**ntelligence **a**rtificielle</span> [FRENCH], Artificial Intelligence Involvement Index [ENGLISH]) has attempted to propose a detailed grading system to distinguish the level of AI involvement in online videos, with the aim of helping video creators, viewers, and industry professionals clearly define and understand the extent of AI involvement in the video production process.

The introduction of this standard has made it feasible to quantify the extent of AI assistance in the creative process. However, the theory still has certain shortcomings, and the author has long failed to update this standard. Therefore, building upon the IIIA framework, I have slightly optimized and refined it to propose this approach.

CVIII (Content and Visualization Index with Intelligence Involved) expands the scope of detailed classification standards for AI (artificial intelligence) involvement to cover all online content, including articles and videos, with the aim of helping online article and video creators, readers, viewers, and industry professionals clearly define and understand the extent of AI involvement in the production process. 

## Problems with IIIA
When developing IIIA, it was assumed that the AI component of the copy and the AI component of the presentation were the same; if there is a discrepancy between the two, the component with the higher AI percentage is used as the overall index.

However, when there's a significant discrepancy between the AI component of the core content and that of the presentation, the problem becomes apparent: a video with a script written by AI (corresponding to Core Content Level 4) but filmed entirely with real people, with all footage created by real people (corresponding to Visualization Level 0), should be rated as 4 under the original standard. But the resulting rating could easily lead to the misunderstanding that “the live-action footage in this video was also ‘generated by AI’”.

To that end, I have slightly optimized and refined the IIIA, giving careful consideration to potential issues and making the necessary corrections. If you find yourself at a loss when applying the IIIA standard due to the issues mentioned above, you may wish to try using this revised version.
  
## Level Determination (Simple Table)
When there is little difference between the core content and the AI component of the content presentation, or when only one of the two is present, the CVIII level may consist of a single digit; in this case, the digit directly corresponds to the degree of AI assistance, and its specific meaning is essentially the same as that of IIIA.

For example: The original reference standard IIIA on which this amendment is based covers only core content and does not explicitly address AI in terms of content presentation. It essentially retains the IIIA rating of `IIIA-1*`. Therefore, the CVIII rating for this proposal is `CVIII-*1` or its abbreviation `CV-*1`.

 | Type            | Level    | Meaning                         | 
 | --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | 
 | AI not assisted | 0        | AI was not used at all               | 
 | AI not assisted | 1        | AI is provided for reference only and not participated in the production | 
 | AI assisted     | 2        | Human-led, with AI used to enhance results                               | 
 | AI assisted     | 3        | All content has been reviewed and screened by humans                     | 
 | AI assisted     | 4        | There exists content that has not been reviewed by humans                | 
 | Generated by AI | 5        | Entirely Created by AI                                                   | 
 | Talk about AI   | \*       | The content itself discusses AI (This type is used when rating the content only)| 
  
## Level Determination (Detailed Table)
If there is a significant disparity between the core content and the level of content presentation, the level component of CVIII is expressed as a two-digit number. The first digit indicates the degree of AI-assisted involvement in the core content, and the second digit indicates the degree of AI-assisted involvement in the content presentation.

For example: Tom uses AI to write a screenplay (corresponding to Content Level 4), but the entire production is filmed with real actors, and the background music and other materials are also created by real people (corresponding to Visualization Level 0). Therefore, under this standard, the rating is `CVIII-40`, or its abbreviation `CV-40`.

 | Level    | Core Content (Concepts, Scripts, Logic) | Visualization (Material Generation, Production, etc.) | 
 | -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | 
 | 0        | **Completely created by humans.** The concepts, scripts, and logical sequences were all conceived by humans, with no AI involvement throughout the entire process. | Created entirely by humans. All images, music, and video footage were either created by hand or shot live, and the editing was done entirely by hand, with no AI involved at any stage. | 
 | 1        | Entirely completed by humans. **AI is used solely as a tool to inspire creativity and provide reference** (e.g. keywords for brainstorming, and trend analysis), and doesn't directly generate opinions. | Entirely completed by humans. AI serves only as a tool to provide supplementary inspiration (e.g. image search suggestions, and music recommendations). | 
 | 2        | The work is primarily done by humans, with **AI providing basic support** (e.g. grammar checking, simple text polishing, fact-checking prompts). | The work is primarily done by humans, with **AI generating a small amount of content** (e.g. background images & virtual scenes). The integration of the content and the core editing are done manually. | 
 | 3        | **AI generates a large volume of content modules** (e.g. storyboards, arguments, and first drafts of scripts), but these undergo in-depth human editing, review, fact-checking, and finalization. **Humans retain the authority to make key decisions.** | **AI generated some of the key content** (e.g. main illustrations, background music, and some video clips), but all content has been rigorously screened, edited, or compiled by humans. | 
 | 4        | **AI drives the entire logical chain of content production** (such as the development of arguments in news commentary and the design of storylines), while humans perform only limited polishing or minor adjustments (such as correcting inappropriate phrasing). | **AI generates most or all of the content**, with human review limited to spot checks. | 
 | 5        | **AI independently generates complete content** without any human intervention in the creative process. | **AI autonomously handles the entire process** of generating, selecting, editing, and cutting all content. Humans do not intervene in the creative process in any way; they only perform operational tasks (such as uploading and publishing). | 
 | \*       | Revise the options: For videos centered on AI, add an asterisk before the core content level (e.g. Level \*1). | — | 
  
## Note
When publishing online content, creators may, based on the actual extent of AI assistance used in production, clearly label the Content and Visualization Index with Intelligence Involved (CVIII) with these standards by using the phrase “CVIII: `{Level}`”,“`{CVIII-AB}`” or “`CV-AB`”. Besides, creators can also display the corresponding level sticker, so that viewers can better understand the video's production process.

If your project includes a `README.md`, you can use the following badge to showcase your project's CVIII level.
